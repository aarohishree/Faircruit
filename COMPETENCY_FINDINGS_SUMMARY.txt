## 🔴 THE BUG - Force Downgrade Logic

**File:** `backend/timed_assessment_system.py`, Lines 267-268

```python
if prediction.predicted_level.value in ["Analysis", "Synthesis", "Mastery", "Influence"]:
    prediction.predicted_level = CompetencyLevel.APPLICATION
```

**Why it exists:** Because professional_rubrics.py doesn't have descriptors for ANALYSIS (level 3) and SYNTHESIS (level 4), the system downgrades them to APPLICATION (level 2) to avoid errors.

**Result:** Candidates can never legitimately achieve levels 3 or 4, even if they qualify.
